Image upload

Drop in an image and get back a URL that renders as a real picture in a Mimir note, and unfurls as one everywhere else. Needs the upload token — unlike every other page here, this one writes to a server.

Open

Paste the first into a Mimir block and it becomes the picture, sized to its real proportions. The second is the bytes — use it as an <img src>, or paste it into Slack. Both are unlisted rather than private: anyone holding the link can see the image.
